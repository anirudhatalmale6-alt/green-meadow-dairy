Our farm

Dairy the way it should be

For over 25 years our family has run Green Meadow Dairy with a simple belief: good dairy starts with well-cared-for animals and honest, small-batch methods. No shortcuts, no fillers.

  • Milk collected and delivered the same morning
  • Curd, paneer and butter made fresh in small batches
  • Ghee hand-churned the traditional bilona way
  • Free from preservatives, colours and added water

Common questions

Do you deliver every day?

Yes — we deliver fresh dairy 7 days a week between 6 AM and 9 PM across the city. You can also set up a recurring daily or weekly order.

How fresh are the products?

Milk is collected and delivered the same morning. Curd, paneer and butter are made fresh daily with no preservatives added.

Is there a minimum order?

There's no minimum, but orders over ₹500 qualify for free delivery. Smaller orders carry a small ₹40 delivery charge.

How do I pay?

This is a demo store, so no real payment is taken. On a live store we can enable UPI, cards, net-banking and cash on delivery.

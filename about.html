Our farm story

Three generations of honest dairy farming — and a promise you can taste in every bottle.

Since 1998

A family farm, run with care

Green Meadow Dairy began with a handful of cows and a simple idea — that families deserve real, fresh dairy without additives or compromise. Today we still follow the same small-batch, animal-first approach.

Our herd grazes on open pastures, and every drop of milk is collected, tested and delivered the same morning. Nothing sits in a warehouse for days.

Our promise

Made the honest way

  • Grass-fed, well-cared-for cows and buffaloes
  • Curd, paneer and butter made fresh in small batches every day
  • Ghee hand-churned using the traditional bilona method
  • No preservatives, no added colour, no watering down
  • Cold-chain delivery so it reaches you as fresh as the farm
